Password resets require identity verification and a reset link. If the customer is locked out after multiple failed attempts, create a support ticket.
Refund requests require the order ID, customer email, and reason for refund. Finance approvals are required for refunds above 1000 RMB.
VPN connection failures should be routed to the network team after confirming the customer's device, error message, and office location.
Account access for VIP customers should be escalated immediately if the issue blocks login for more than 30 minutes.
Invoice correction requests must include the original invoice number, updated company information, and the business justification.
